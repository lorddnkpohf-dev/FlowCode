# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Send a private report through GitHub's security advisory or contact the project owner directly.

Include:

- Affected version.
- Steps to reproduce.
- Potential impact.

We will respond within 7 days and coordinate disclosure.

## Scope

- The application and its official installer.
- The public website and release assets.

## Out of scope

- Third-party dependencies used as-is.
- Social engineering or phishing.
- Physical access attacks.
